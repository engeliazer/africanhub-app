Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Introduction

At The African Hub, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

Last Updated: March 15, 2024

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We collect the following personal information to provide and improve our services:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and mailing address
  • Account Information: Username, password, and account preferences
  • Payment Information: Payment method details and transaction history

Device and Usage Information

We automatically collect information about your device and how you interact with our platform:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, browser type, and IP address
  • Usage Data: Login times, pages visited, features used, and time spent on the platform
  • Location Data: General location information based on IP address
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: To enhance your browsing experience and analyze platform usage

How We Use Your Information

Primary Purposes

  • Account Management: To create and maintain your account, process your transactions, and provide customer support
  • Service Delivery: To deliver our educational services, track your progress, and personalize your learning experience
  • Communication: To send you important updates, notifications, and educational materials
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, identify areas for improvement, and enhance our services

Secondary Purposes

  • Marketing: To send you information about new courses, features, and promotions (with your consent)
  • Analytics: To generate anonymous, aggregated statistics about platform usage
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes
  • Security: To protect our platform, users, and prevent fraud or abuse
Note: We will never sell your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data for analytical purposes.

Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security assessments and updates
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms
  • Secure data storage and backup procedures
Important: While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

Your Rights and Choices

You have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your information (subject to legal requirements)
  • Data Portability: Request transfer of your data to another service
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of marketing communications and certain data processing

To exercise these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@ocpac.com

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes
  • Enforce our agreements

When we no longer need your information, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
